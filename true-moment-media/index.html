About Us

THE STORY BEHIND TRUE MOMENT MEDIA

At TrueMoment Media, we believe every moment has a story worth telling. We are a creative videography and media production brand dedicated to capturing life's most meaningful experiences through beautiful visuals, professional production, and cinematic storytelling.

Born in Lagos and driven by a passion for unforgettable moments, we work with couples, families, artists and brands across Nigeria — bringing a calm, professional presence to every set and a meticulous eye to every frame.

  • Our Creative Vision

    To make cinematic storytelling accessible to every Nigerian story — from intimate ceremonies to nationwide campaigns.

  • Our Commitment to Quality

    Professional cameras, crisp audio, careful lighting and polished edits — quality you can see and hear in every delivery.

  • Our Storytelling Approach

    We listen first, then film with intention — shaping real emotions into films that feel honest, elegant and timeless.

  • Our Passion

    Nothing beats the feeling of handing someone a film they will replay, share and treasure for years to come.

TrueMoment Media was founded on a simple conviction: the moments that matter most deserve more than a quick recording. They deserve craft. From the first consultation to the final colour grade, we treat every project as a collaboration — understanding your vision, your people and the feeling you want to preserve.

Whether it is the quiet tear at a traditional wedding, the energy of a Lagos concert, or the story behind a growing brand, we bring the same standard: deliberate composition, clean sound, cinematic movement and an edit that respects the truth of the moment. That is the TrueMoment promise.
