## Recommended sizes

- Hero / CTA backgrounds: **1920px** wide, JPEG quality ~80.
- Service & portfolio thumbnails: **1200px** wide.
- Keep each image under ~250 KB where possible (use Squoosh, TinyJPG or similar).

## Replacing an image

Keep the same filename to avoid editing HTML, or update the `src` attribute in
`index.html`. Always write descriptive `alt` text for accessibility and SEO.
